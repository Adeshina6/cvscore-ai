Transparency

No black box — here's what we check

Six specific things every scan evaluates and reports back to you, with no guesswork.

Parseability

Can an ATS extract your contact info, work history, education, and skills? We parse your file the same way and flag anything that gets lost.

Formatting

Multi-column layouts, tables, text boxes, and graphics break most ATS parsers. We identify the specific issues in your file.

Keyword match

Paste a job description and we compare it against your resume line by line — showing exactly which required terms are absent.

Readability

Weak verbs, vague bullets, and missing measurable outcomes reduce your impact. Each bullet gets reviewed individually.

Skills alignment

Gaps between your skills section and the target role, grouped by technical skills, soft skills, certifications, and tools.

0–100 composite score

All six dimensions roll into one score with a full breakdown, saved to your dashboard so you can track progress between versions.
